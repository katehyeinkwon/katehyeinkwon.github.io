--- layout: archive title: "Teaching" permalink: /teaching/ author_profile: true --- Having taught nearly 1,600 students across in-person and online courses at the University of Georgia (UGA), I have learned that students enter the classroom with different backgrounds, confidence, and needs. These experiences have shaped my teaching philosophy, which is grounded in the idea that students learn and grow most when two conditions are present: a classroom where they feel safe, heard, supported, and valued, along with clear structure and guidance that prepare them to exceed expectations beyond the classroom. The same principles guide my work outside of formal courses. As advisor for the UGA Girls' Club: Women in Sport Chapter, I mentor undergraduate students pursuing careers in the sport industry and support their professional development by organizing guest lectures and networking opportunities.
